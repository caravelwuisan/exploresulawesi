🏝️ Destinations

Explore the Region

Four worlds within reach — from world-class diving to ancient volcanic landscapes, all connected by a single gateway city.

Bunaken

Coral walls, green turtles, and some of the best diving on Earth. UNESCO-calibre marine park, 30 minutes from Manado.

🐠 Diving Paradise 📍 30 min

Manado

The vibrant gateway to North Sulawesi. Waterfront dining, bustling markets, Chinese temples — and the only international airport in the region.

✈️ International Hub 🍜 Food Capital

Tomohon

Volcanic highlands, the famous flower market, and Lake Linow's tri-colour waters. A cool escape 1 hour from Manado.

🌺 Volcano Trekking 🏔️ 850m Elevation

Lembeh Strait

The global capital of muck diving. Frogfish, pygmy seahorses, blue-ringed octopus — critters found nowhere else on Earth.

🔬 Macro Paradise 🏆 World #1
